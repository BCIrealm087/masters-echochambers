# What interaction and language can reveal about political communities

**Integrated thesis direction · 2026-11-26 · Design synthesis**

Do account groups learned from earlier reposts predict whose material people amplify later? Do linguistic repertoires add information about that behavior, even among accounts expressing similar positions on the same political events? These are the two linked questions for the Computer Science thesis. The repost study establishes the behavioral baseline. A focused Brazilian Portuguese study tests whether patterns in hashtags and authored language describe a group, reveal boundaries that its repost network misses, or improve prediction beyond prior ties, topic and stance. ## Procedures that link the stages

1. **Keep the units and meanings explicit.** A hashtag co-occurrence edge describes expressions appearing together. An account–feature edge describes an account using a reference or rhetorical form. A repost edge records one account amplifying an original author. A language-similarity edge records similarity between profiles. Keep authorship, quoted speech and amplified material attributable to their respective speakers. Report results per authored post, per participating author, per distinct amplified original and per repost event. Check recirculated text, copied slogans, dominant accounts and missing context.
2. **Preserve independence in both time and evidence.** M3’s frozen repost groups give the language study a behavioral starting point. Develop repertoire families and models on development data. Use separate later posts and blinded reference coding to evaluate them. Where possible, separate authors, originals and near-duplicates when testing whether a classifier recognizes a repertoire across people. Train each rolling fold using its own earlier data and freeze feature selection, graph weights, eligible cases and outcomes before evaluating. A period used to choose features, comparisons or thresholds becomes development evidence for that decision. Register a fresh untouched evaluation period for confirmation.
3. **Use matched comparisons to explain a gain.** For the language prediction test, compare the model using history, frozen repost membership, topic and stance with the same model plus repertoire features. Fit each with pre-cutoff information. Use identical scored actions and candidate sets for paired log scores and report differences in coverage before restricting to a common cohort. Break out previously unseen sharer–author pairs with established profiles. M3 scores the realized author conditional on a recorded repost and uses a retrospective author candidate set. The selected item's text can describe that action after scoring. A prospective item-choice task would need content for all eligible candidates at event time and a documented availability rule. [Repertoire prediction design](linguistic-repertoire-hypothesis.md#where-language-enters-prediction)
4. **Keep the criteria for interpreting political evidence.** Excess internal reposting supports a conclusion about observed amplification. Independently coded stance and content can establish political coherence and congruence. The project’s term *selective-sharing echo-chamber candidate* additionally requires an independently supported comparison with available opposing material. Private trust, exposure and belief change require their own observations. [Conceptual criteria](reflexive-report-8.md)

## What would change the thesis conclusion?

If membership improves prediction beyond prior ties, the interaction partition contributes information about later source choice. If membership adds little with adequate precision, the result limits what the partition adds beyond familiar behavior. If repertoires differ after matching and recur across authors, there is evidence about how political positions are expressed. If the differences disappear after holding topic or stance comparable, or after removing copied items, the claim narrows to topic, stance, or amplification of particular materials. If repertoire features improve later predictions under the matched comparison, language contributes behavioral information. Distinct repertoires can also cut across repost groups and support a substantive discourse finding of their own.

An internally amplified post can also have been discussed across a group boundary. If this recurs across many sharers and originals, the same communities can be cohesive in whom they amplify and porous in whom they address. Interpreting the exchange as political agreement or antagonism would require independently attributed Portuguese text from the original, quote and reply.
