# Do frozen repost communities predict later sharing beyond habitual ties?

**Incremental reflexive review · Report 5 · 2026-11-25**

How much excess internal reposting survives controls for account activity, source popularity, collection context and prior sharer–author relationships? Keep the October temporal holdout. Make the primary result one estimate across all eligible actions, then ask which groups, sources and relationships account for it. Three days of persistence would be evidence about observed allocation in this collection. Political alignment needs independently coded content or stance. Restricted exposure needs information about what people saw, and causal reinforcement needs a design that identifies an effect of interaction.

## Decision rules, power and uncertainty

Use one upper-tail global randomization test. Adopt the conversation's proposed one-sided α = 0.05 for the directional pilot and freeze it before evaluation. This explicitly changes the previous upper endpoint of a central 95% null range, approximately a 0.025 upper tail. Prespecify the eligible family of secondary group tests, apply Holm adjustment and publish every disposition. Exploratory false-discovery-rate analysis under arbitrary dependence can use Benjamini–Yekutieli if declared separately. Alternate nulls, partitions and subgroup definitions are sensitivity checks.

| Planning default | Interpretation and check |
| --- | --- |
| 100 resolved events from 20 assigned sharers per group | Screen local reporting for usable counts. Report effective as well as nominal participation. At a known 50% baseline and one-sided 0.025, the review's independent-binomial illustration gives 13.4% power with 100 events for an increase to 55%. About 786 independent events first reach 80% in that illustration. Neither figure calibrates a network test. |
| +5 percentage points | Use as a provisional practical screen. Show sensitivity at +2.5, +5, +10 and +20 points and report the baseline. Requiring the observed effect to clear +5 gives about a 50% chance of clearing that gate for a true +5 effect at large sample sizes. To establish an effect above +5, plan against a larger alternative such as +10 and justify effect uncertainty. |
| 70% resolved targets per candidate context and interval | Historical engineering screen, retired by [Report 7](reflexive-report-7.md). Judge coverage using stratified loss and robustness of the intended estimate. For otherwise eligible recorded events from assigned sharers, let I and E count known internal and external outcomes and U count unresolved authors. The possible internal share runs from I/(I+E+U) to (I+U)/(I+E+U), undefined for an empty denominator. At 70% resolution its width is 30 points. These bound the raw share. To assess Δ, recompute the reference under each specified completion or improved lookup. |
| Central null range | Describes variation across random allocations. Give the randomization p-value separately. The finite resolved window has an observed Δ. Generalization requires a stated population and uncertainty model. Report sharer-, author-, post- or dyad-aware sensitivity as appropriate, discovery instability and missingness separately. Three days cannot support campaign-wide temporal uncertainty from a day bootstrap. |

Calibrate false positives and power by simulating **whole event ledgers** with pre-holdout inputs. Include the exact null, unequal activity, viral posts, repeated ties without added affinity, day/query shocks and bursts, group-specific endpoint loss, membership error and an effect concentrated in a hub. Inject a range of group affinities at fixed margins where feasible. Score the entire decision procedure, including group eligibility, Holm and the practical screen. About 1,000 independent simulated datasets per scenario gives Monte Carlo standard error near 1.3 points at 80% power. Use several thousand for borderline false-positive rates and record simulation uncertainty. These are proposed budgets.

Validate the sampler before interpreting p-values. In an unrestricted uniform assignment with M slots, n from sharers in group g and K destinations in g, its internal count has a hypergeometric distribution with parameters M, K and n. Check means and tails on small enumerable cases. For constrained swaps, check exact margins, structural zeros, reachability, acceptance and mixing from multiple starts. A stuck stratum supplies no randomization contrast. For independent uniform draws report (b+1)/(B+1), with b draws at least as extreme as observed. The conversation proposes 9,999 draws initially, subject to tail precision after multiplicity correction. Correlated swap draws require separate Monte Carlo error diagnostics. Use analytical or aggregated unrestricted calculations first. Full permutations require work proportional to events × draws. Benchmark wall time and peak memory on the bounded slice.

## What could overturn the interpretation?

| Proposed interpretation | Prespecified check and consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Frozen communities predict internal allocation | A precise effect below the meaningful threshold limits the substantive claim. A nonpositive effect or compatibility with the primary null in adequately informative data weakens persistence. A wide range compatible with substantial excess is inconclusive. |
| Excess extends across the collection and across members | Refit the matching reference separately by day and context, with one day omitted, under equal-sharer weighting and for distinct pairs. Report maximum shares and effective counts (inverse sum of squared shares) for sharers, authors, posts and dyads. Repeat after dropping leading dyads and the top one, five and 1% of posts and authors separately. Collapse after removing one item or account confines the result to concentrated amplification. |
| Membership adds information beyond familiar relationships and old material | Test dyads unseen in discovery and genuinely new originals where source dating permits. Score history alone versus history plus membership. Little predictive gain limits the additional role of group membership even if the pooled randomization rejects. Separate first observed in the archive from first published. |
| The result survives incomplete observation | Stratify resolution by day, query, group, sharer activity and referenced original. Vary unknown destinations toward internal or external, rerun on matched populations and give the smallest plausible change that reverses the conclusion. Persistent cohort or query differences limit generalization beyond that frame. |
| The result persists through the campaign | Replicate the frozen protocol on an untouched later window. Keeping the original memberships tests persistence of those groups. Rolling rediscovery tests repeatability of the method. Failure after the debate period narrows the temporal claim. A different collection frame can separately test observation bias. |
| The groups are politically selective | Test independent stance or content evidence with its coverage. Weak alignment narrows the finding to structural circulation. Reposts alone leave endorsement, feed exposure, belief, trust and causal influence unresolved. |
