## Search record

The scholarly web search on 2026-11-24 comprised 17 English and Portuguese queries. The pass examined the five returned entries for each query, 85 entries in all before deduplication across queries. This shallower search per query follows the approach used in Step 3, with separate exact-method, topic and language queries plus targeted source and citation lookups. The log uses counts before deduplicating by identifier or title. No date filter was applied. Domain filters, where used, are part of the query text. Twelve research sources or local precedents were retained below, plus the two implementation references. The review is limited to the alternatives needed to design a pilot. ## Citation tracing and source checks

- Calder et al. explicitly build on Zhu et al.'s Laplace learning. Followed that link backward to the harmonic objective and used the later paper as a forward reference for the low-label scenario. Zhou et al. also directly compare their method with a harmonic Gaussian baseline and show different errors as the number of labels changes. [Zhu et al.](https://cdn.aaai.org/ICML/2003/ICML03-118.pdf) · [Zhou et al.](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2003/file/87682805257e619d49b8e0dfdc14affa-Paper.pdf) · [Calder et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/calder20a/calder20a.pdf)
- Cann et al. describe and test projection weightings, including a Newman-style fractional weighting. Followed the connection back to Newman's affiliation-network work and forward to the assessment of long-tailed graphs. [Newman](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.64.016132) · [Cann et al.](https://doi.org/10.1017/nws.2020.11)
- Rechecked the versioned prior-study source to observe which method it calls. Used GraphLearning and NetworkX documentation to verify effective choices supported by candidate implementations. The prior run's version remains unverified. No implementation or package version is selected by this review. [Prior source](https://github.com/Interfaces-UFSCAR/Code-Hashtags-IEEE/blob/c39f1743d9e72cfb156e67d086a93c9cb2c034d0/hashtags_label_spreading.py#L20) · [GraphLearning](https://jwcalder.github.io/GraphLearning/ssl.html) · [NetworkX](https://networkx.org/documentation/stable/reference/algorithms/generated/networkx.algorithms.community.louvain.louvain_communities.html)

## Evidence extracted

| Source | What it studies or specifies | Relevance and limitation for ITED-Br |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Zhu, Ghahramani and Lafferty, 2003](https://cdn.aaai.org/ICML/2003/ICML03-118.pdf) | A weighted graph on labeled and unlabeled examples. Extends fixed labels by a harmonic solution, with experiments on several classification data types. | Defines the method-level baseline. The quality of co-occurrence as a similarity relation and coverage of label-constrained components need checking. |
| [Zhou et al., NeurIPS](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2003/hash/87682805257e619d49b8e0dfdc14affa-Abstract.html) | Symmetric normalization plus repeated mixing of neighbor scores and the initial label signal. Compares methods on toy, digit and text data. The relative results vary with the dataset and number of labels. | Supplies a soft-clamped alternative that can share the same input adjacency as GFHF. The original feature-based graphs and empirical results do not establish a likely winner for campaign hashtags. |
| [Calder et al., 2020](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/calder20a.html) | Analyzes low-label failure of Laplace learning and compares Poisson learning on semi-supervised benchmarks with very few labels. | Justifies a contingency if components or frequency bands have too few seeds. The source does not establish that the original ITED-Br label rate is low enough to cause that failure. |
| [Newman, 2001](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.64.016132) | Weighted coauthorship graphs projected from shared papers, with the per-pair contribution divided by the number of other authors. | The same weighting can model the per-tag influence of one post. The analogy suggests a graph variant and leaves its stance performance for testing. |
| [Cann et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.1017/nws.2020.11) | Compares seven projections of bipartite affiliations on social-media data and artificial graphs with known community structure. Reports sensitivity to weighting, especially for long-tailed network structures. | Supports a controlled comparison of weights and retaining the incidence data. The paper tests community detection and does not measure candidate-relative stance. |
| [UstanceBR](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10579-025-09896-3) | Brazilian Portuguese Twitter/X resource with around 46.9 thousand tweets covering six stance targets plus unclear cases. Includes text, timelines and network identifiers, with in-domain and cross-target evaluations. | Useful annotation and transfer example. Unit, topics and sampling differ from normalized hashtag–candidate labels in ITED-Br. |
| [Christhie et al., 2018](https://homepages.dcc.ufmg.br/~fabricio/download/brasnam2018.pdf) | Brazilian Portuguese campaign tweets in the 2018 election, with semi-supervised construction of stance data and evaluation of conventional text classifiers. | Shows a local precedent for automated labeling. Its label sources and tweet-level predictions are separate from held-out validation of hashtag labels here. |
| [Shchur et al., 2018, preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.05868) | Empirical GNN evaluation across shared benchmarks. Changing label splits changes the ranking of algorithms. | Motivates repeated paired splits and consistent model-selection rules. Direct transfer of model rankings falls outside this review. |
| [Leicht and Newman, 2008](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.100.118703) | A modularity formulation that uses edge direction and the separate in- and out-degrees. | Baseline objective for repost communities. On its own, optimizing this objective measures the graph used for discovery. |
| [Rosvall and Bergstrom, 2008](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0706851105) | Map equation partitions weighted directed networks by compressing random walks. | Supplies a flow-based algorithmic comparison on the same adjacency. Random walks on the repost graph are a model of that graph, with insufficient evidence to establish actual pathways of exposure. |
| [Interian and Rodrigues, 2023](https://doi.org/10.1088/2632-072X/acf6a4) | Directed repost graph of the 2022 Brazilian election, constructed using election-related keyword queries and a multilevel community method. | Provides a direct local comparator and an example of scale. It does not estimate the size of an ITED-Br slice using different query contexts and join rules. |
| [Prior hashtag-network study](../prior-study.md) | Candidate-specific 2022 hashtag co-occurrence graphs and Laplace propagation, scored from hashtag use at account level. The [public code](https://github.com/Interfaces-UFSCAR/Code-Hashtags-IEEE/tree/c39f1743d9e72cfb156e67d086a93c9cb2c034d0) reads hydrated data and label workbooks. | Gives the primary comparisons and continuity of time period. The original source manifest, labels, seed selection and exact historical version limit replication. |
