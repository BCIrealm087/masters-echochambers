# What do Brazilian election communities write and what do they amplify?

**Incremental reflexive review · Report 2 · 2026-11-25**

Do account communities identified through reposts differ in the stances and rhetorical practices expressed in their members’ own Portuguese text? Within each community, how does that writing compare with the material members repost? The thesis can test these questions if it can recover text and context across independently identified groups and establish which labels people can apply reproducibly. A group can write little hostile material and still circulate one hostile post many times. These are different observations about the same community.

This unit integrates the researcher-supplied 13-section conversation review (*Pasted text(1).txt*) and the eight-page Deep Research review (*Literature Review_ Brazilian Portuguese Political Discourse.pdf*). The conversation discusses the repository directly. The PDF reports that it worked from the described aims and omitted its search evidence table. Both are targeted reviews. The [Q3 evidence notes](m1-q3-evidence.md#reflexive-report-2-evidence-addendum) distinguish the original search, additional sources and transfer limits. This is a design decision. Text recovery, human agreement, classifier performance and group contrasts await empirical work.

## 1. Determine which questions the available material can answer

Start after the structural pilot defines the account population. Freeze the partition independently of language labels and record its version, unassigned accounts and instability. Establish a permitted, reproducible text source. Record query memberships, interval and time convention, selection rules, and the availability of authored contributions, original source text, reply parents, quote boundaries and relevant media. Charged expressions used to collect posts can shape the sample before analysis starts. Restrict conclusions to observed query contexts and dates. [ITED-Br feasibility](m1-ited-br-feasibility.md) · [Collection paper](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0316626)

Produce a coverage table by group, day, post type and query, with separate rows for unique sources and repost events. Count eligible units, recoverable texts, attributable speakers and targets, sufficient context, and codable units for each outcome. For shared material also record unresolved source IDs and how much event weight they carry. If one group disproportionately lacks reply parents, examine the comparison within a common covered subset and report exclusions. For a binary tactic, bound the eligible-frame proportion by assigning all unknown cases first absent and then present, using design weights where applicable. If a contrast depends on those assignments, report the covered subset or the bounds. If no suitable text is recoverable, the deliverable is the coverage audit.

## 2. Measure what each speaker contributes

Use the [revised codebook and Portuguese boundary examples](m1-political-language-taxonomy.md). Keep six linked layers: speaker/target/context, stance, communicative function, rhetorical mechanism, factual proposition, and behavior. The author’s contribution supplies the primary stance record for each target. Retain quoted speakers separately, mark whether a proposition is attributed, questioned, asserted, endorsed or refuted, and attach the shortest supporting span. Preserve the original Portuguese spelling, quotation boundaries and emoji. Normalize a separate copy for retrieval and duplicate detection.

Benoit’s campaign functions motivate praise, criticism and defense. Framing and persuasion research adds *how* the speaker constructs the argument, including appeals to authority, threats, identity and moral evaluation. Distinguish an evaluation of a particular TSE decision from a challenge to the legitimacy of the electoral process. Likewise, an expression of indignation establishes observable language; audience emotion or private distrust requires separate evidence. Use praise, policy advocacy or criticism, character attack, defense, correction, mobilization and reporting as candidate core functions. Pilot finer mechanisms individually. Keep culturally important but unreliable distinctions available for qualitative interpretation.

For exact-claim checking, retrieve candidate fact-checks and verify equivalence of speaker, proposition, date, scope, quantity and negation against historically applicable evidence. Record the evidence and reason for each verdict. Count checkable, selected, checked, unresolved and contradicted propositions separately. Estimating falsity beyond the verified subset requires probability-based verification and an account of unverified cases. A quote rebutting a false allegation and a plain repost carrying that allegation have different authorship records. Only independent behavioral evidence can support an inference of coordination, and intent or control requires additional evidence. Detailed verification and coordination studies are optional extensions.

## 3. Establish an independent reference before automating

Draw a probability sample of authored posts across groups, periods and post types, preserving inclusion probabilities and author IDs. Include low-activity authors through an account-based or two-stage design when estimating the average author. Draw a second sample covering ordinary as well as prominent amplified sources, retaining repost multiplicities. Build a separate challenge set for rare codes, implicit targets, irony, quotes, reused slogans and missing media. Its deliberately difficult mix serves error diagnosis.

| Stage | Proposed design and decision |
| --- | --- |
| Calibration and pilot | The conversation suggests 100–150 diverse calibration items followed by 150–250 independently coded pilot posts. The PDF suggests a 5% pilot and a 10% expert audit. Size these exercises from label rarity, precision and timed local effort. A fixed percentage of a large archive is a poor workload estimate. |
| Human reference | As a planning scenario, allow 600–1,000 eligible main-sample posts and 150–250 separate challenge items. Recruit two Brazilian Portuguese readers with 2022 election expertise across the team. Obtain independent labels on a substantial shared subset, preferably the full held-out set. Hide graph assignments and machine suggestions during independent judgment. Preserve original labels, spans, uncertainty reasons and adjudication. The existing 16 expert-review examples test the review procedure. |
| Reliability | Report prevalence, raw positive and negative agreement, nominal Krippendorff’s alpha by stance or binary tactic, confusion tables, span agreement, missing context and adjudication rates. The PDF’s κ around 0.60 as a revision signal and the conversation’s α near 0.80 as an aspiration serve different purposes. Rare labels need enough positive observations and uncertainty estimates. Merge, redefine or retain qualitatively any label whose boundary reviewers cannot reproduce. |
| Model comparison | On identical splits, compare rules for aliases and explicit cues, TF-IDF with a linear classifier, general Portuguese BERTimbau, Twitter-trained BERTabaporu, and zero-/few-shot LLM annotation using a frozen Portuguese prompt with span evidence and abstention. Multilingual encoders are a transfer comparison. Embeddings support duplicate detection and claim retrieval. UstanceBR supplies an external stance benchmark. |
| Evaluation | Keep authors, source originals and near-duplicates separated across training and evaluation where feasible. Document conflicts and test transfer to another period. Tune thresholds on development data. Report held-out per-label precision/recall, macro-F1, calibration and accuracy versus retained coverage. Inspect errors by group, candidate, post type, activity and context. Check whether changing party cues changes judgments on otherwise equivalent examples. Use humans for unresolved attribution and consequential verdicts. |

## 4. Compare groups using four explicit denominators

For each frozen group, tactic and period, calculate the following on the relevant eligible, codable units. Publish numerator, denominator, sampling weights, uncertainty interval and missing share. For stance, condition on a particular target and distinguish indeterminate judgments from mentions with no expressed evaluation. Multi-label tactics can co-occur.

| Quantity | Calculation and interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Authored-post prevalence | Divide weighted authored posts containing the tactic by weighted codable authored posts. Count a tactic once per post. Measures the mixture of the group’s written output. |
| Equal-author prevalence | Compute each eligible observed author’s within-author proportion, then average over authors with account selection weights where needed. Report contributing authors and those with no codable contribution. Measures the average participating author’s mixture. |
| Distinct-source prevalence | Within each sharing group, count each distinct codable original once. Divide originals carrying the tactic by all such originals, with sampling weights if sampled. Measures breadth in the group’s source repertoire. |
| Repost-event prevalence | Divide weighted deduplicated repost events linked to originals carrying the tactic by weighted repost events with codable originals. Measures prominence in observed circulation. Multiple shares of one original can dominate this rate. |

Report between-group differences in percentage points for each quantity and within-group differences between authored and amplified mixtures. Tabulate source-origin group by sharing group where both are known. Compare raw results with estimates standardized to a common day, topic, target and post-type mix only where comparable strata overlap. October 16–21 includes the October 16 runoff debate and the following day’s discussion peak. Examine event-day and leave-top-source-out results. Use account-clustered uncertainty for authored posts and account for both shared originals and repeated sharers in repost estimates. Six days provide little independent information about between-period variation. Repeat comparisons on feasible alternative partitions and use independent human validation to quantify group-specific classifier error and propagate its uncertainty. If correction is unstable, report the human-sample estimate or the unresolved contrast.

## 5. Decision for the thesis

The minimum credible extension is a text-coverage audit, a frozen compact codebook and independent reference sample, a matched model benchmark, then the four descriptive comparisons and bounded expert interpretation. Outputs answer five questions: candidate-relative stance, rhetorical practice, amplified composition, evidential quality of selected checkable claims, and reliability of the measurements themselves. Preregister non-directional contrasts. Negligible differences, disappearance after topic/day adjustment, source dominance, uneven missingness or systematic disagreement each change what can be concluded. Retain those outcomes in the research record.

The [structural pilot](m1-advisor-synthesis.md#proposed-first-pilot) remains first. Once credible language estimates exist, the [Report 1 evaluation](reflexive-report-1.md) can use the same frozen aggregates, attribution, uncertainty and coverage in all explanation formats. Human judgment is needed for independent annotation and interpretation. Sampling, record linkage, comparison tables and reproducibility checks can be scripted.
