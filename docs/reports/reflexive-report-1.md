# Can grounded Portuguese prose improve the interpretation of political account communities?

**Reflexive review · Report 1 · 2026-11-25**

Keep the thesis centered on discovering account communities and independently testing their observed repost circulation. Treat LLM-assisted interpretation as a secondary experiment. Its research question is whether, given identical evidence, generated prose helps a reader reach more accurate conclusions, recognize limits or audit claims faster than carefully written deterministic prose. A negative result would settle a real design choice and leave the network study scientifically useful.

## What the review changes

M1 proposed comparing a fixed-format summary with grounded Portuguese prose and asking two political scientists to review selected cases. That comparison could reward the convenience of reading sentences. Add a deterministic Portuguese prose control, measure what readers can do, and evaluate how verification changes the generated output. Retain the M1 structural evidence, speaker distinctions and independent expert judgments. This revision governs the explanation study when suitable evidence exists. The [public-data pilot](m1-advisor-synthesis.md#proposed-first-pilot) still comes first.

| Format | What readers receive from the same frozen records | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Structured evidence | Table of measured values, qualifiers, provenance and available examples | Auditable reference |
| B. Deterministic Portuguese prose | Conditional sentences reporting direction, missingness, conflicting specifications and limits, with evidence references | Primary comparator for C |
| C. Grounded LLM prose | Short Brazilian Portuguese synthesis restricted to the packet, with references for each factual assertion | Experimental condition |
| Optional follow-up | Software fills verified values while a model selects and connects approved statements. A separately authored expert description can provide a human reference. | Test the hybrid if attribution or numeric errors recur. Budget any human authoring separately from evaluation. |

Match information, examples, access to sources, required qualifications and reading budget across arms. Compare C with B for generation-specific benefit and B with A for the effect of prose presentation. A measured perturbation card, annotated flow diagram or ranked source table may explain hub dependence or unstable membership directly. Give all arms access to the same structural evidence when used. A model has no role in setting membership, validating the holdout, assigning independent stance labels or producing fact-check verdicts.

## Evidence and scope of inference

For each case, freeze the query slice, discovery and holdout windows, graph specification, partition version and stability, actor-to-original-author direction, internal/external/unresolved counts, denominator, reference expectation, sensitivity checks and material limitations. Mark absent fields explicitly. Attach language evidence only when access, use, speaker, target, sampling and coverage permit it. A candidate-centered audience, a news event or one dominant source can concentrate sharing. Political alignment requires independent evidence. Exposure, endorsement, trust, belief, coordination and intent each require additional observations appropriate to the claim.

**Fictional example from the conversation.** Of 120 reposts, 100 have resolved destinations and 72 are internal. Against a 48% reference expectation for the resolved events, the observed excess is 24 percentage points. Assigning the 20 unresolved events to either extreme gives an all-event internal share between 60% and 76.7%. Those bounds cover the observed share only. The expected share would have to be recomputed under the corresponding scenarios before interpreting the excess across all events. A reader who notices this distinction has gained something measurable from the explanation.

Register each evidence item with an ID, provenance, population, period, unit, value and qualification. Link each atomic claim to supporting items and score it as supported, partial, contradicted or unverifiable. Check the full paragraph as well. Several true statements about a politician, a source and a repost pattern can jointly imply a motive that none establishes. A valid citation ID alone earns no grounding credit. Verify counts, rounding, dates, direction, denominators, names and required qualifications programmatically where possible. Use independent semantic review for entailment, political context, selective omission and speaker attribution. A model judge may flag cases for review after calibration.

## Evaluation and adversarial cases

1. **Freeze the comparison.** Before viewing outputs, specify eligible cases, strata, exclusions, required facts and limits, critical errors, comparison arms and primary reader tasks. Keep prompt/template development and calibration separate from evaluation. Sample real cases across size, activity, source concentration, missingness, stability and mixed evidence. Record selection and eligibility. Politically named strata require independent labels. Keep a diagnostic challenge set separate from a representative sample used to estimate performance.
2. **Test controlled changes.** Change one denominator, reverse an edge or a reference comparison, remove a hub, weaken the holdout or remove a reply parent. Include concentrated news audiences, heterogeneous stance, quote rebuttals, irony, viral repetition by a few accounts, conflicting graph specifications, and an imperfect fact-check match. Swap candidate cues in otherwise equivalent synthetic cases to test symmetry. Include valid IDs attached to unsupported claims and quoted instructions that must be treated as source text. Score appropriate abstention as well as correct positive conclusions.
3. **Audit both stages.** Save each raw generation, the checked version, rejected cases, corrections, fallback to the table and correction time. Report strict support among all factual assertions, required-fact and consequential-limitation coverage, numeric/direction accuracy, correct abstention and critical errors per document. A serious unsupported political inference blocks release of that version. Keep failed outputs in the experimental denominator. Zero observed errors in a small set leaves substantial uncertainty.
4. **Measure reader performance.** Randomize conditions and case order, hide method labels, use neutral case IDs and common styling, and assess whether readers guessed the source. Give each reviewer only one format per case before the first-pass questions. Rotate formats across readers to compare conditions on matched cases. Ask readers to identify the correct denominator, interpret holdout versus discovery, detect a planted error, locate the supporting item and name an inference the evidence cannot establish. Record answer, evidence ID, time and confidence before discussion. Evaluate confidence against accuracy. Readability and preference are secondary.
5. **Preserve expert disagreement.** Use the [Portuguese review form](m1-expert-validation.md) with explicit ratings for precisão factual, fundamentação, completude, clareza, neutralidade, adequação ao português brasileiro and utilidade explicativa. Calibrate on separate examples. Preserve independent ratings, reasons and evidence IDs. Report item-level agreement and counts, with ordinal Krippendorff’s alpha when repeated ratings and sample size support it. Audit arithmetic objectively, repair unclear coding rules on fresh cases, and retain competing interpretations or an indeterminate verdict when evidence is insufficient. A median may summarize ratings. Excluding disputed cases or counting an adjudicated majority as independent agreement would distort the result.
6. **Analyze at case and reader level.** Estimate matched contrasts with uncertainty accounting for repeated reviewers, cases and generations. Report performance by evidence quality and failure type. With very few reviewers or cases, present descriptive differences. Atomic claims from the same document cannot supply independent document-level replication.

## Scale, thresholds and decision

The conversation proposes 4–8 real packets, 10–20 synthetic challenges and two independent reviewers for feasibility. The PDF suggests starting with 3–5 clusters, then sampling about 10–15 communities with at least three Brazilian political scientists and overlapping ratings. A four-case start fits both. The historical M1 allowance of 94 minutes per reviewer covers several tasks, including four paired explanation cases. Time the revised three-format tasks separately. Two reviewers can diagnose failures but cannot cover three formats independently on every case in a first-pass design. Balance incomplete assignments at feasibility scale. Before expansion, budget actual assignments (cases × formats × ratings per output), calibration, verification and adjudication, and recruit enough independent readers for the intended overlap.

The conversation estimates 4–6 total external reviewer-hours for a small explanation pilot and 12–25 for an expanded evaluation, excluding the underlying network and text work. Its formal-study planning range of 30–60+ real cases and 4–8+ reviewers is conditional on pilot variability, reader burden, available cases and a precision or power analysis. The two sources offer planning scenarios, not established sample requirements.

Candidate targets from the conversation include 98% fully supported factual claims, 95% required-fact coverage and 95% correct abstention, with complete critical-limit coverage, accurate numbers and direction, valid references and zero critical errors in released material. Example worthwhile gains are 10 percentage points in comprehension or 20% less audit time at comparable accuracy. Measure feasibility and variability, then justify and freeze thresholds ahead of confirmatory testing. Prefer task accuracy and error recognition to a change in judgment alone.

| Outcome of an independent comparison | Thesis disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Meaningful, reproducible gain over deterministic prose on comprehension, verification or recognition of conflicting evidence, with grounding and confidence calibration preserved | Consider promoting grounded explanations to a larger contribution |
| Better readability or convenience alone, comparable performance, or a stronger deterministic result | Report the comparison and use the simplest supported presentation |
| Too few validated cases, inadequate evidence, unavailable independent reviewers or recurrent consequential errors | Defer the affected experiment. Structural reporting can proceed on eligible evidence. |

## Novelty, provenance and outstanding evidence

Prior Brazilian election work already studied community structure, polarization and network reach. Interpretable community detection also offers structural explanations without generated prose. The plausible contribution is a controlled test of auditable political interpretation under real measurement limits. Priority or first-use claims would require a systematic full-text review.
