# Search and evidence notes for LLM-assisted group explanations

**M1 · Step 6 supporting notes · 2026-11-24**

These notes document the Q4 search from the [M1 review protocol](m1-review-protocol.md). The [Step 6 report](m1-llm-group-explanations.md) uses the findings to design a comparison of a fixed summary and LLM prose grounded in the same ITED-Br evidence.

## Search record

Seventeen focused English and Portuguese scholarly web queries were run on 2026-11-24. Five entries per query underwent title or snippet screening, for 85 entries before deduplication. This continues the shallower per-query depth used for Steps 3–5, with multiple terminology and domain variants and follow-up checks in the primary sources. No date filter was applied. The log records counts before deduplication. Relevant papers were checked for the task, unit and meaning of factual or explanation fidelity. Searches did not produce a direct benchmark for explaining Brazilian election account communities from a fixed evidence set. The closest cases inform specific components of the planned evaluation.

## Citation tracing

- Followed citation and generation evaluation from Gao et al. to the later RAGTruth paper, which references that work and demonstrates why retrieval-based generation still needs checked outputs. Separately reviewed the atomic-claim and identified-source concepts to make the comparison’s units explicit. [Gao et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.398/) · [Niu et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.585/) · [Min et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.741/) · [Rashkin et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2023.cl-4.2/)
- Traced the network question through the explainable community-finding study and reused the consensus clustering work consulted for Step 3. Looked forward to an LLM-based GNN explanation study to check whether model fidelity is the same evaluation task. It measures fidelity to predictions. For ITED-Br the priority is interpreting a measured group partition and its validation evidence. [Sadler et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/s41109-022-00515-6) · [Lancichinetti and Fortunato](https://doi.org/10.1038/srep00336) · [Baghershahi et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1944/)

## Evidence extracted

| Source | What it evaluates | Relevance and limitation for ITED-Br |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Sadler et al., 2022](https://doi.org/10.1007/s41109-022-00515-6) | Repeated community-finding experiments, using network features and feature importance in supervised models to study group assignments and uncertainty on benchmark graphs. | Supports a measured explanation of grouping. A feature associated with membership in a benchmark provides insufficient evidence of why an ITED-Br participant engaged in a particular way. |
| [Lancichinetti and Fortunato, 2012](https://doi.org/10.1038/srep00336) | Combines repeated partitions using how often vertex pairs are grouped together. | Can motivate explaining a group's stability with repeated-run evidence. Stability measures reproducibility under the stated perturbation. |
| [Baghershahi et al., 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1944/) | LLM explanations of graph neural network predictions using graph context and model embeddings. Measures fidelity, subgraph size and human-facing qualities. | Helpful boundary for the model-explanation question. Its fidelity target concerns a trained predictor, unlike the held-out sharing analysis. |
| [Gao et al., 2023](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.398/) | ALCE evaluates answer quality and citation support and distinguishes statements supported by citations from irrelevant citations. | Motivates testing both whether a statement is sourced and whether every cited record is relevant. Adapting the scheme requires checking graph measures and cross-record comparisons. |
| [Rashkin et al., 2023](https://aclanthology.org/2023.cl-4.2/) | Attribution to identified sources evaluated by annotators for question answering, summarization and table-to-text. | Supports inspecting whether evidence entails a claim as written. It leaves the quality and representativeness of the underlying ITED-Br evidence to the analysis. |
| [Min et al., 2023](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.741/) | FActScore decomposes generations into checkable atomic information and estimates the supported share, with a study of biographical generation. | Good scoring unit for precise claims. Include a required-fact measure as well, since supported precision alone can reward an incomplete explanation. |
| [Niu et al., 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.585/) | RAGTruth annotates hallucinations at case and word levels in responses generated with retrieved material. | Motivates marking unsupported or contradicted spans and checking excerpts as well as numbers. Benchmark rates do not predict performance in Portuguese political analysis. |
| [Parikh et al., 2020](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.89/) | ToTTo uses highlighted Wikipedia table cells for controlled one-sentence English generation. Documents outputs that introduce unsupported material. | Motivates the identical-input comparison and explicit validation of denominators and claims. Genre and language differ. |
| [Scalercio et al., 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1193/) · [Cunha et al., 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.propor-1.74/) | Portuguese sentence simplification assessed quantitatively and qualitatively, and end-to-end Portuguese retrieval and generation for hospital documents. | Illustrate evaluation in Portuguese and contrasting tasks. Neither source establishes how well a model will handle political quotation, sarcasm or locally specific implications. |
| [Zheng et al., 2023](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/91f18a1287b398d378ef22505bf41832-Paper-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.pdf) | Compares language-model judgments and human preferences, and examines order, length and judge-related biases. | Motivates randomized presentation and independent review where meaning is in question. A model judge can assist triage, with no assumed accuracy on this rubric. |
