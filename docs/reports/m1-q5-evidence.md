## Citation tracing

- Followed Artstein and Poesio forward to Oortwijn et al., which explicitly cites its treatment of agreement measures and proposes documented procedures for addressing disagreements. Read Mostafazadeh Davani et al. alongside that procedure to distinguish repairable annotation faults from genuine interpretive differences. [Artstein and Poesio](https://aclanthology.org/J08-4004/) · [Oortwijn et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2021.humeval-1.15/) · [Mostafazadeh Davani et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2022.tacl-1.6/)
- Used the general guidance on political measurement and purposeful sampling to plan selection of near misses and a limited number of diagnostic cases. Regional studies supply examples of group and stance annotation. The planned case mix and time estimates are proposals for this project. [Grimmer and Stewart](https://doi.org/10.1093/pan/mps028) · [Palinkas et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10488-013-0528-y) · [Ferreira et al.](https://doi.org/10.26694/2317-3254.rcp.v13i2.6933)

## Evidence extracted

| Source | Contribution | Transfer and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Grimmer and Stewart, 2013](https://doi.org/10.1093/pan/mps028) | Principles and examples for validating automated political text analysis in the specific application. | Domain reviewers can check construct interpretation and whether a code works in context. Graph holdout, missingness and robustness need their own evidence. |
| [Palinkas et al., 2015](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10488-013-0528-y) | Discusses purposeful case selection and how selection strategies fit different qualitative and mixed-method goals. | Inspires a case mix with contrasts and weak evidence. The design is for diagnosing problems and has limited value for population estimates. |
| [Ferreira et al.](https://doi.org/10.26694/2317-3254.rcp.v13i2.6933) · [Furlani and Mitozo](https://doi.org/10.26694/2317-3254.rcp.v12i2.6000) | Brazilian 2022 election studies use coded discourse from heavily shared group posts or a candidate campaign account. Ferreira et al. describe three coders, and Furlani and Mitozo document a codebook. | Shows how to preserve language and electoral context. The original case selections do not estimate coding reliability for this project’s broader task. |
| [Brazilian political-elite stance corpus](https://aclanthology.org/2025.nlp4dh-1.32/) | Separate relevance, stance and sentiment annotation dimensions in a Brazilian Portuguese dataset, with structured training and reported agreement. | Supports distinctions in the codebook and the use of calibration examples. Vaccination discourse and the population differ from election communities. |
| [Artstein and Poesio, 2008](https://aclanthology.org/J08-4004/) · [Feinstein and Cicchetti, 1990](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2348207/) | Reviews coder reliability and shows how prevalence or marginal imbalance can complicate interpretation of a chance-adjusted measure. | Report pre-adjudication counts, confusion and both-positive/one-positive/both-negative signals. The planned sample is too small for a stable global coefficient to be required. |
| [Oortwijn et al., 2021](https://aclanthology.org/2021.humeval-1.15/) | Structured resolution with analysis of what caused disagreement and changes to the instructions. | Preserve first ratings and log why an ambiguity was resolved or the task changed. Genuine ambiguities can remain open. |
| [Mostafazadeh Davani et al., 2022](https://aclanthology.org/2022.tacl-1.6/) | Studies subjective language labels and the limitations of collapsing individual reactions to majority votes. | Keep alternate readings and use multidimensional outcomes. The project uses different labels and a smaller expert-review design. |
| [Rashkin et al., 2023](https://aclanthology.org/2023.cl-4.2/) | An annotation framework for deciding whether generated claims are attributable to identified sources. | Evaluate semantic support separately from readability. The source measures attribution and does not establish that the original source is representative. |
| [van der Lee et al., 2019](https://aclanthology.org/W19-8643/) · [Belz et al., 2020](https://aclanthology.org/2020.inlg-1.24/) | Human evaluation practice and design properties for generated text. Discusses choice of audience, order, criteria and reporting. | Counterbalance formats and record case order, review criteria and first-presentation effects. Two experts can diagnose failures. Estimating reader preference more broadly would require additional readers and cases. |
