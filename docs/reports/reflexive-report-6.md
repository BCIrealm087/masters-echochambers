# Which repost communities persist across graph constructions?

**Incremental reflexive review · Report 6 · 2026-11-25**

Which groups of accounts emerge from Brazil’s 2022 election reposts, and which conclusions depend on how we build the graph? Use directed, count-weighted Leiden as the preferred discovery method once its implementation passes a directed-objective check. Retain Louvain as the reproducible baseline, Infomap as a flow-based comparison, and a degree-corrected directed stochastic block model (SBM) as the first model for distinguishing cohesive groups from shared source roles. Select and freeze the primary partition on discovery evidence. [Report 5](reflexive-report-5.md) governs subsequent inference.

This unit integrates the original graph-methods question and the 17-page *Comparative literature review of graph methods for political-community and candidate echo-chamber detection*. ## What would weaken the interpretation?

- **Unstable or selectively assigned groups.** On common assigned accounts report variation of information, adjusted mutual information and adjusted Rand index. Align labels and report per-group Jaccard, splits, merges, components and size distributions across seeds and date perturbations. Alongside these comparisons give node, event, resolved-target and assigned-sharer coverage for each method. A small group can disappear while aggregate agreement stays high.
- **Dependence on repetition, hubs or election events.** Loss of a bloc under binary weights narrows the claim to amplification intensity. Remove the highest in-strength sources and refit discovery to see whether a group survives. Separately remove hub-associated held-out actions under the original frozen membership and recompute the matched reference. Suggested discovery-ranked removal fractions are 0.01%, 0.05% and 0.1%, plus prespecified candidate/media sources. Record rounding, ties and actual numbers removed. Report each perturbation separately, including omission of the 17 October debate-related peak.
- **Apparent persistence limited to prior items, ties or collection context.** Apply Report 5’s global primary test on the common eligible event ledger, conditioning on day, disjoint provenance, sharer activity and original-post popularity. Identified authors outside the partition count as external. Report unresolved bounds on corresponding denominators. Give percentage-point excess, null interval and Monte Carlo precision, followed by adjusted local comparisons. Check prior ties, new originals and an untouched replication period. Degree/activity-stratified label permutations and time-shifted labels can serve as negative controls. Different quote/reply mixing would narrow the inference to the repost layer. [Statistical protocol](reflexive-report-5.md)
- **Insufficient political evidence.** A stable partition identifies structural groups. Held-out excess supports selective observed amplification under the stated reference. A candidate political echo-chamber pattern additionally requires independent candidate-relative evidence and excess within-stance sharing against an appropriate cross-stance comparison. Report mixed, news-oriented and uninterpretable groups when supported. Feed exposure, trust and belief reinforcement require evidence beyond repost records. [Independent stance evaluation](reflexive-report-4.md)

## Reproducibility and computational scope

Begin synthetic recovery on directed, weighted LFR graphs with 10,000 nodes, heterogeneous degrees and group sizes, varying mixing from easy to weak separation, with 20 replicates per configuration. Scale selected configurations to 100,000 nodes. Include a heavy-tailed source-popularity stress test and a directed block model containing cohesive political blocks and a common media/source role. Record recovery by planted role and degree band. For later extensions, use planted overlap (5–20% dual-membership nodes), popular versus group-specific items in a bipartite model, or temporal switches and event shocks. Score overlap recovery or transition precision/recall where appropriate. These are controlled tests of known structures. The election data supply no comparable ground-truth partition. [Directed LFR benchmarks](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.80.016118) · [Degree correction](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.83.016107)

For each run retain input version and hash, event rules, graph and partition hashes, counts and exclusions, package/compiler versions, CPU, settings, seed, objective, group count, elapsed time and peak resident memory. Measure join, construction, clustering and randomization separately. At the comparator’s 1.2 million accounts and 8.5 million arcs, 64-bit sparse indices and weights alone occupy about 146 MB including row offsets. Reverse adjacency, library objects and repeated fits raise actual memory use. The review budgets 16–32 CPU cores and 32–64 GB RAM as a planning envelope. Measure the bounded pilot before scaling. Report 5 governs the simulation budget and sampler checks for inference. Keep identifiable ledgers and memberships in local research storage.

The minimum pilot can proceed with verified directed Louvain, its binary sensitivity and directed Infomap. Prioritize a verified Leiden comparison and a frozen holdout before expanding to the SBM, user–post discovery or temporal layers.
